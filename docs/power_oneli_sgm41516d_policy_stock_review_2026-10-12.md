# Power third-round: SGM41516D identity, Motorola dual-battery policy and public-stock evidence

**Date:** 2026-10-12. **Owner:** Power (`razr/power`). **Target:** Motorola razr 2022 `oneli`, stock ADB observed XT2251-1 PVT / hwrev 0xC000 / SKU dn, Android 14 `U2SLS34.1-42-14-11`. **Evidence types:** pinned Motorola source, separate Motorola-family driver comparator, public stock archive metadata, and a brief physical stock ADB capture. **No kernel driver/charging writes, no phone currently accessible.**

**Integration sequencing:** (1) separately expose read-only BQ and main GLINK data after normal kernel driver validation; (2) validate *variant-specific* SGM41516D register behavior, regulator and enable/IRQ resets; (3) implement/port dedicated paired manager with conservative fail-off behavior and isolated synthetic policy tests; (4) only after phone access and measured response, enable charging and load switches. No mainline `sgmicro,sgm41542` fallback/alias in DT before (2). No direct charge-setting or inductor-power path experiments without the hardware plan.
