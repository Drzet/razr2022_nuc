### L2B / PTN3222 request: 8 mV rounding is not a development blocker

- [Isolated accepted PTN3222 driver](https://github.com/Drzet/linux/blob/1e5268277f6d4c25cde2f3e2b3f00439107e087a/drivers/phy/phy-nxp-ptn3222.c) lines 66–85 requests `regulator_set_voltage(vdd3v3, 3075000, 3300000)` after first enabling/voting S10B at 1.8 V; OFF uses `regulator_set_voltage(..., 0, 3300000)` and `regulator_disable` in the opposite order. This 3.075 V minimum is a **driver request**, not a proven BUK silicon minimum. It is **3,000 µV above** the vendor source's 3,072,000 µV initial setting.
- Mainline PM8350 `ldo2` uses `pmic5_pldo` (1,504,000 µV + 8,000 µV × selector). Thus **3,072,000 µV is selectable (selector 196), but does not satisfy a 3,075,000 µV lower-bound vote. The first selectable value inside [3,075,000, 3,300,000] is 3,080,000 µV (selector 197).** 3,080,000 is 8,000 µV higher than the *initial stock vote*, still within the stock *allowed range* 2,700,000–3,300,000; no hardware voltage is measured.
- [NXP's published PTN3222 **CUK** Rev 1.2 datasheet, operating table 31 p.23](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/data-sheet/PTN3222_CUK.pdf) supports VDD3V3 **2.85–3.63 V**, VDD1V8 **1.62–1.98 V**; [WILLSEMI WAS7227Q original datasheet rev1.4](https://www.omnivision-group.com/files/product/files/WAS7227Q_V1.4.pdf) supports VCC **2.5–5.5 V**. These documents support the *generic family and AUX* voltage overlap, **not** the precise **PTN3222BUK** variant's operating window or the safety of changing its stock rail.
- **Engineering recommendation (non-blocking):** keep the accepted PTN `[3075000,3300000]` µV request unchanged. On PM8350 L2B it selects **3.080 V**, just **8 mV / 0.26%** above the stock 3.072 V initial vote. This is a routine 8 mV grid-rounding discrepancy, not a credible reason by itself to delay USB/DP development, modify regulator-core rounding, or lower the PTN request to 3.072 V. The schematic and original vendor vote independently establish that the board was intended to operate in this approximately 3.07 V region. The published CUK data supports an appropriately broad family operating range, though does not establish exact BUK electrical limits. **Missing BUK-specific documentation is retained as a provenance gap, not an additional voltage-gate prerequisite.** Do not claim 3.080 V was measured or physically validated; normal board-integration and on-device tests remain necessary. Active native hardware remains disabled for its *separate* unfinished provider/USB/DP/lifetime and integration gates, not because of these 8 mV.
- **Proposed provider constraint:** retain vendor-permitted PM8350 L2B `regulator-min-microvolt=<2700000>`, `regulator-max-microvolt=<3300000>`; do not fix rail at stock initial 3.072 V, because it contradicts an enabled PTN's extant 3.075 V minimum. Set the consumer upper bound consistently to 3.3 V. WAS7227Q does not need an independent 3.075 V vote. This constraint/request proposal is ready for a later Boot/USB/DP implementation review **without further research solely into the 8 mV difference**.

### AUX power lifetime and smallest reusable driver change

- GPIO-SBU upstream [`gpio-sbu-mux.c`](https://github.com/Drzet/linux/blob/2b5acf108ae37040ac1e652e029bb37f5609e886/drivers/usb/typec/mux/gpio-sbu-mux.c) has Type-C orientation/mode switch callbacks and a high/disconnected active-low OE_N mapping, but **no regulator acquisition**, voltage vote or enable at all. Its [binding](https://github.com/Drzet/linux/blob/2b5acf108ae37040ac1e652e029bb37f5609e886/Documentation/devicetree/bindings/usb/gpio-sbu-mux.yaml) already permits `vcc-supply`. A board phandle alone **does not** energise U4500. A PTN3222 `phy_power_on/off` power vote is session-scoped and **must not** be the AUX retention owner.
- **Owner decision:** `gpio-sbu-mux` should acquire and own `vcc-supply` directly **if the property is present**, using the **existing generic regulator consumer API**, not a separate GPIO, fixed regulator, new switch driver or AUX PTN PHY coupling. Require `vcc-supply = <&oneli_l2b>` for the later WILLSEMI board node. Use `devm_regulator_get_optional(dev, "vcc")` only for property-present devices, or a strict `devm_regulator_get` in that branch, **never accept a dummy supply** for the oneli node. Do not change older users without `vcc-supply`.
- **Minimal lifetime semantics:** acquire regulator before registering Type-C mux/switch; enable before making the switch available and before any GPIO can assert the active-low OE_N; then **keep a distinct regulator enable reference while the AUX device is bound**, across DP attach, orientation changes, normal USB detach and PTN PHY power-off. The GPIO callback toggles **OE_N/SEL only** and never gates this rail. On remove/error, assert OE_N disabled, unregister Type-C mux/switch, then release the regulator enable reference. A managed regulator-disable action registered immediately after successful enable ensures error unwind after each subsequent GPIO/typec registration failure. On failure to get/enable the supply, abort probe, do not register Type-C switch/mux and never fabricate `vcc`.
- **Suspend qualifier:** the existing PM8350 RPMh driver sends **`RPMH_ACTIVE_ONLY_STATE`** votes. A device-lifetime enable reference holds L2B while the CPU active and AUX is bound, but does not **by itself** prove L2B output persists through full RPMh sleep/collapse. No accepted wake-capable DP system-suspend policy exists. A future suspend/DP wake requirement needs supported regulator sleep votes or a separately proven firmware-owned rail and quiescence tests; don't use `regulator-always-on` as a substitute. The proposal therefore covers *normal detach and PTN off*, while **suspend-retention remains an explicit integration hold**.
- **DP delta:** retain already accepted `compatible="willsemi,was7227q", "gpio-sbu-mux"`, vendor-prefix addition (DP owner), `enable-gpios=<&tlmm 2 GPIO_ACTIVE_LOW>`, `select-gpios=<&tlmm 71 GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH>`, `mode-switch`, `orientation-switch`, connector `port@2` ↔ AUX independent of PS5169, with source-backed shared `vcc-supply=<&oneli_l2b>`. Binding addition *alone* is not sufficient: a minimal **generic driver regulator-management delta** is required before Boot can enable that node; Power only specifies its contract, no code change is authorised now. Keep FSA4480 disabled.
