## Source-backed product and register conclusions

Stock six-FDT export: [run 38220276125](https://github.com/Drzet/razr2022_nuc/actions/runs/38220276125), inner JSON SHA256 `1a07f09d9ce7c3c4c1bcd4317ef634b28197a93bffe464e1c63f737579063465`. Identical **27, not 29** PS5169 triples in all four overlays. PVT candidate overlay index 3 board ID `0x45/c000`, **not known selected**. Downstream Motorola source [`ssusb-redriver-ps5169.c` blob `01ca89030bfd830c777d96efe67e8d0889235e5c`](https://github.com/MotorolaMobilityLLC/kernel-msm/blob/0450c5076a8ec9415a10888c8c183ef6ff5f91b8/drivers/usb/misc/ssusb-redriver-ps5169.c) drives **active-high GPIO19 enable**, holds high across operation with 10ms settle, does not use it as pulsed reset. NXP at 0x4f is the reference-selected repeater (S10B/L2B symbolic supplies), Diodes at 0x6c explicitly disabled, PS5169 at 0x30 uses VCC S10B, VIO L8C. External NXP exact SKU and on-phone applied overlay unverified.

Vendor PM policy gates power-down to USB/USB+DP, retains enabled DP-only and upon resume replays **entire profile** and mode. Mainline candidate holds rails+enable across system sleep with **no active suspend/resume implementation**. Power cycling without established wake ordering/DP-only retention/profile replay is a deliberate hold. When revisiting, require mode/DP state ownership, regulator retention behavior, mutex protection, full profile/mode replay after power restoration, error propagation and dirty-cache retry. Do not attempt generic 1ms GPIO19 reset pulses or copy vendor UCSI notifier semantics. GLINK remains sole owner of orientation GPIO91; standalone DP AUX is separate GPIO2/GPIO71 hardware.
