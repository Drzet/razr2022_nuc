## Bounded read-only battery/thermal plan

1. **Qualcomm main pack GLINK:** the pinned source audit [8 matching opcodes, 41 property IDs](../power_oneli_charging_protocol_2026-10-11.md) supports initial read-only *GET* requests `BATTMGR_BAT_PROPERTY_GET=0x30`, `BATTMGR_USB_PROPERTY_GET=0x32`, and optionally `WLS_PROPERTY_GET=0x34` once firmware replies are observed. Mainline `qcom_battmgr` automatically sends notification **subscription `0x04`** on service-up. This is a protocol registration request, *not a battery charging command*. Actual ACK payload length compatibility remains untested. Do not claim fully passive bus traffic.
2. Mainline also exposes **writable** charge thresholds calling `BATTMGR_CHG_CTRL_LIMIT_EN=0x48` from its property setter. This opcode is not established for oneli vendor firmware; for a *strict read-only diagnostic configuration* ensure the threshold setter path and all user-triggered supply writes are **not exposed/called**. Inspect actual upstream probe initialization and PMIC GLINK match before enabling even reports. No source claim of automatic `0x48` probe write: pinned implementation sends notification `0x04` at service-up; charge-control `0x48` occurs through explicit property setter.
3. **Flip pack:** stock ADB proves separate BQ27426 on I²C `0x55`; generic upstream `bq27xxx` may provide readouts, but exact oneli profile `NM40_ATL_712MAH`, current polarity inversion and external thermistor `mmi,temp-source=1` policy are **not** automatically reproduced. Initially treat generic profile/temperature and charge counter as unverified until probe side-effect audit and reference register comparison. No automatic profile-update writes in diagnostic phase.
4. **SGM41516D @0x6b** remains **disabled**: no confirmed exact oneli driver binary/variant ID, CE PM8350C GPIO2 and PM8350B GPIO1/3 load-switch fail-safe or paired-temperature/OCV policy. The Motorola-family comparator is not proof of safe 41542 alias. No charger write or auto enable. `mmi_battery` aggregate and cross-pack thermal control do not exist under generic mainline; present two independently named supplies first, never an invented healthy aggregate.
5. **Thermal:** expose only justified read-only PMIC/ADC, main GLINK temperature and gauge temperature if relevant driver/probe and sensor channels validate. Keep stock Motorola temperature-zone/current throttling held; a temperature in deci-C from individual batteries does not validate the external thermistor placement or combined highest-pack health reporting.

**Next acceptance:** coordinator combined **Image/modules + normal modpost** including standalone temperature fix, Boot's distinct schema/FDT checks, and read-only diagnostic runtime only when handset unlocked/available. No native boot, electrical voltage, charger behavior, phone thermal mitigation, PS5169 link or stock selected overlay has been tested by this Power owner.
