2. **New exact numerical result:** all **12** A730 HFI `SH0/MC0/ACV` encoded DDR table entries in the pinned kernel match compiled Motorola stock values by index. For B=compiled vendor bandwidth at nonzero index i: `SH0=0x60000000|(B//1050)`, `MC0=0x60000000|(B//800)`, `ACV=0x60000008`; index0 all `0x40000000`. This establishes a **deterministic table-index crosswalk**, unlike our superseded observation that equal cardinality was insufficient. Mainline A730 **still does not dynamically select** nonzero HFI bandwidth indices because `info->bcms` is absent and `a6xx_gmu_set_freq` initializes `bw_index=0`. Future narrow 0x07030002-specific selection design can re-use exactly verified 12 HFI rows, but needs a validated OPP/index ABI, firmware/command-DB semantics and transition ownership. Do not activate by copying raw vendor index into an unrelated new generic BCM field.

Existing GMU CX votes are GMU-frequency-derived from `cx.lvl` rather than per-GPU-frequency `qcom,cx-level`; source and firmware metadata still do not prove who enforces stock CX minima. Do not inflate GX OPP values or invent physical RPMh votes.
