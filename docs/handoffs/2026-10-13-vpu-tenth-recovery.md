## Changes and external provenance

**Major policy finding:** Vikash Garodia et al., **[PATCH v5 00/13] media: iris: Migrate iommus to iris sub nodes**, 2026-09-26 Message-ID `20260926-vpu_iommu_iova_handling-v5-0-0322ca5dc10c@oss.qualcomm.com`, [series](https://lists.openwall.net/linux-kernel/2026/09/26/358). Directly overlaps the experimental peer-compatible driver, binding, lifetime and allocator. The upstream authors use video-controller **`non-pixel`/`pixel` subnodes**, ordinary platform DMA devices, and proposed **`iommu-ranges`** ([01 binding](https://lists.openwall.net/linux-kernel/2026/09/26/359), [04 core API](https://lists.openwall.net/linux-kernel/2026/09/26/362), [05 context device](https://lists.openwall.net/linux-kernel/2026/09/26/350)). Maintainer review specifically rejects no-reg reserved-memory-only `iommu-addresses` as an upstream ABI and favors device-side `iommu-ranges`. Kernel tip of `torvalds/linux:master` inspected 2026-10-13 still lacks these changes; **pending v5, not merged**. v5 targets VPU3x+, explicitly leaves VPU2 migration for later: **oneli VPU2/Gen2-specific adaptation remains ours**. Unrelated 2026-09-15 [VPU2 HFI Gen2 4-pipe patch v2](https://lists.openwall.net/linux-kernel/2026/09/15/2174) offers additional reuse. Mobile out-of-tree/postmarketOS search did not identify a closer maintained implementation; coverage bounded to indexed public sources, not all repos. Vendor `LowTension/android_kernel_xiaomi_sm8475@39382bf760ccf05a8fb4dddee7138ecac02d4a59` comparator remains documented.

**Disposition: adapt upstream v5; do not expand the parallel peer-compatible implementation.** Experimental eight kernel commits remain on branch for review/benchmark only; do not integrate custom `qcom,sm8475-iris-nonpixel/pixel`, parent `qcom,dma-consumers`, no-reg pure IOVA-only reserved-memory ABI. No destructive rebase, no unapproved upstream claim. A provenance-preserving, isolated v5 foundation backport with oneli/VPU2 delta is the narrow replacement. Leave full buffer routing, OPP/ICC and board enablement for later review.

**Actual code completion this turn:** fixed a failed *schema assertion* in `tools/testing/iris/schema-test.py` to accept `False schema does not allow` from the validator rejecting forbidden parent `iommus`, kernel commit [`a6327db1`](https://github.com/Drzet/linux/commit/a6327db1c2dbeab9d18ba913c20cb126a3a5a1a1). ## Shared files / next acceptance test

Next **implementation** should reuse source-pinned v5 subnode binding (and dt-schema #207 dependency), standard child platform devices, and upstream-originated `iommu-ranges` support before adapting oneli's two stock SIDs/masks `0x2180/0x400`, `0x2187/0x400` and conservative stock IOVA windows. Keep pixel floor `0x00100000` explicitly [INFERRED] as stock-vs-v5 discrepancy, upper exclusion `0xe0000000`, PAS 7MiB physical carveout separate, and SM8475 V4L2 gated until separate routing authorization. VPU2-specific context-creation hooks differ from v5's vpu3x target and need host-reviewed adaptation. Accept only after attributed patchset, schema and relevant object compilation are reproduced on the actual new kernel head. Boot can integrate shared oneli DTS **only** after that review.

No new device-only blocker, native firmware ACK or permission step is needed for the offline work. Coordinator should update the tenth queue to reuse this existing series rather than requesting further bespoke consumer ABI development or another generic DMA audit.
